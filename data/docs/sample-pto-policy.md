# Northwind Time-Off Policy

## Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of PTO per year, accrued monthly at
1.67 days/month. Unused PTO rolls over up to a cap of **10 days**; anything
above the cap on December 31 is forfeited.

PTO requests of 3 days or more should be submitted at least **two weeks** in
advance through the HR portal. Your manager approves PTO; approval is expected
within 3 business days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive **10 days** of paid sick
leave per year. Sick leave does not roll over and is not paid out on
termination.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth
parents receive **8 weeks**. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the
birth or adoption.

## Holidays

Northwind observes 11 company holidays. In addition, every employee gets
**2 floating holidays** per year to use for any occasion. Floating holidays
expire at year end and are not paid out.
